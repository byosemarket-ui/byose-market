How to use BYOSE Market

1. Create or sign in to your account

Use Sign Up to create an account, or Sign In with your email/phone and password. Stay signed in to keep your session active on this device.

2. Manage your account

  • Profile — name, contact details, and photo
  • Address Management — saved delivery addresses
  • Reset Password — change your password securely
  • My Preferences, Language, and Notifications

3. Shop and checkout

Browse products on the storefront, add items to your cart, then checkout with a saved or new delivery address. Choose an available payment method (for example Cash on Delivery or online payment when enabled) and confirm your order.

4. Follow your orders

Open My Orders to see each order’s status (for example Pending, Paid, Processing, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Cancelled, or Completed). Open an order for product details and delivery information.

5. Cancellation, returns, and refunds

Use Cancellation & Refunds to review the official policy and submit eligible requests for your own orders. Admin reviews requests; refunds are not marked complete until processing is confirmed.

6. Log out

From Settings, tap LOG OUT, or use Logout from My Account. This ends your session on this device. You can sign in again anytime.

Frequently asked questions

  • Open My Orders, select the order, and review its current status and delivery details on the order page.
  • Eligible orders can be cancelled within the policy window before dispatch. Go to Cancellation & Refunds and use the cancel action when it is available for that order.
  • Manage saved addresses in Address Management. Choose a saved address during checkout. For an order already placed, contact support as soon as possible.
  • Open Reset Password while signed in, enter your current password, then choose a new strong password.
