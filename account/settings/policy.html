Cancellation & Refunds

Official BYOSE Market cancellation and refund rules, plus actions for your own orders. Requests are reviewed by BYOSE Market — this page never invents a completed refund.

  • Customers may cancel an order within 48 business hours after placing it, provided the order has not yet been dispatched.
  • If an order is not delivered within 48 business hours after confirmation, and the delay was not communicated in advance, the customer may request a full refund.
  • If the customer receives a different product from what was ordered, the company may replace it or issue a full refund. Notify BYOSE Market within 24 hours of receiving the order.
  • If the delivered product is significantly different from its description or unsuitable for its intended purpose, the customer may request a return within 24 hours. The product must be unused and in its original packaging.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 24 business hours after the returned product has been received and inspected.
  • Refunds are made through the original payment method whenever possible.
  • The product has been used.
  • The product has been damaged by the customer.
  • The product is returned without original packaging.
  • The request is outside the stated time limits.

Final decisions are made by BYOSE Market after review. Submitting a request does not automatically approve or reject a refund.

Business days

Business days are Monday through Saturday. Sundays and official public holidays are excluded.
